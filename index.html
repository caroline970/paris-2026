Airport → the Marais: take the taxi €56 flat

Paris taxis run a fixed fare from CDG — €56 to anywhere on the Right Bank, set by decree. Not metered, no airport surcharge, no traffic risk, covers both of you and all the luggage. Use the official taxi rank, never anyone who approaches you inside the terminal.

The RER B is €14 each (€28 for two) but means a change at Châtelet and hauling bags up stairs after a red-eye. You're paying €28 to skip that — worth it on arrival day. ⚠️ The Roissybus no longer exists — it was retired 1 March 2026, so ignore any guide that still lists it.

77950 Maincy

2,000 candles, champagne bar, stilt walkers, fireworks to close. Every Saturday May–Sept — your Aug 1 is one of them.

Getting there Direct château shuttle from Cour Saint-Émilion (métro line 14), out ~4:30 PM.

✅ BOOKED 29 July — €94 total. Sale no. 63509706837685568109, under Caroline Ulstrup. Four e-tickets in ETickets.pdf; digital is fine, no need to print.

🚌 Bus stop: Terroirs de France, Avenue des Terroirs de France — beside métro Cour Saint-Émilion (line 14). Out 4:30 PM. Show the ticket to the driver. Return leaves the Vaux car park at 11:40 PM and comes back to the same stop.

🕕 Your 18:00–18:30 slot is only for starting the château interior — the gardens and the carriage museum you can enter the moment you arrive. The estate is open 11 AM to midnight (last entry 9:30 PM), so nothing is wasted getting there at 5:30.

⚠️ No cloakroom and exits are final. The château says plainly it has no consigne, so carry in everything you need at once — picnic, layers, umbrella — and make it disposable.

💡 Not as locked-in as it looks: the tickets are non-refundable but the date is changeable in your profile under “Mes billets” up to the day before, through 26 September.

🧺 Bring your own picnic — it's allowed. There are designated picnic areas by the canal (map handed out on site), and alcohol is permitted, so bring a corkscrew. Two rules that matter for you: exits are final (no popping out and back), and there are no lockers or bag check — so since you're on the coach and not in a car, make everything disposable. You don't want to carry an empty cool bag around for six hours and onto the 11:40 shuttle.

🛒 Where to buy it: Bercy Village, right at the shuttle stop. Cour Saint-Émilion is a pedestrian street of shops and traiteurs at the top of the métro steps — so you buy the picnic 45 minutes before boarding instead of carrying food around museums all day. Leave lunch by 3:15 to give yourselves time to shop.

Buy the wine there too — and a cheap corkscrew, since you can’t fly with one. Skip the château’s catering entirely.

Getting home is settled: the château's own return shuttle leaves Vaux at 11:40 PM and puts you back in Paris ~12:15 AM — after the 11 PM fireworks. Book it with the entry as one round-trip ticket. (The taxi-to-Melun escape route exists if you wilt, but it costs you the fireworks.)

Part 2 Must-dos & little joys

✨ See the Eiffel Tower sparkle

Every night it glitters for five minutes on the hour — and in midsummer the first show is around 11 PM. Your Thursday boat glides right past it. To catch the twinkle another night, watch from Pont de Bir-Hakeim, where the arches frame the tower and the crowds thin out.

Pont de Bir-Hakeim, 75015 Paris

🥐 The croissant crawl

Three worth every calorie:

  • Tout Autour du Pain — in the Marais, steps from home base. Your everyday spot.134 Rue de Turenne, 75003 Paris
  • La Maison d'Isabelle — two minutes from Notre-Dame. Famously the best croissant in the city, just €1.40 — grab one Sunday around Mass.47ter Boulevard Saint-Germain, 75005 Paris
  • Du Pain et des Idées — by the Canal Saint-Martin. A 24-hour-rise legend; pair it with a canal walk.34 Rue Yves Toudic, 75010 Paris

🚶 Long walks, the right way

You love to walk — so do it when Paris is coolest: before noon or after 6 PM, never the 2 PM heat. The best routes:

  • Marais → Île Saint-Louis → Notre-Dame along the river — your Sunday morning.Île Saint-Louis, 75004 Paris
  • Berges de Seine, the car-free riverbank, at golden hour.Port de Solférino, 75007 Paris
  • Canal Saint-Martin — leafy, local, and croissant-adjacent.Quai de Valmy, 75010 Paris

🍫 One sweet splurge

A box of macarons from Pierre Hermé to split on a bench in the Place des Vosges. That's the whole activity.

Pierre Hermé: 72 Rue Bonaparte, 75006 · Place des Vosges, 75004
